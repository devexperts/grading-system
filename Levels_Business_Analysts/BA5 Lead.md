# Lead Business Analyst

Lead BA is a catalyst to delivery of solutions of any complexity. Lead BA can kick off the most complex projects and onboard new teammates to ensure further project development. Lead BA is improving requirements management practices company-wide and creating a positive image for Devexperts BA practice.

Lead BA is the backbone of Devexperts Solution Delivery. Along with Solution Architects, Lead BA is playing a key role to set up a project, to formulate and address business needs of customers at any project stage, and to showcase the advantages of Devexperts products to cover those needs.

Lead BA is referenced as an example, and is the escalation point when something goes off rails. Lead BA knows how to get the answers and how to push the required processes. Lead can feel the balance between underperforming and goldplating, and can make difficult choices between speed and quality.

Typically, Lead BA would cover activities related to presale, leading teams of BAs on larger projects or onboarding them to smaller ones, while still doing the routine work corresponding to Middle and Senior grades..

Further development for a Lead is always a personal discussion, since every situation is unique.

## Overview and regular tasks
- I negotiate with clients complex business cases and design solutions for them. I have a proven track of completed projects.
- I can partially substitute Project Manager and Solution Architect (T-Shaped skills)
- I establish and adjust BA practices company-wide. I am mentoring other BAs.
- I am delegating tasks and overseeing workload of other BAs in my team.
- I plan and execute internal and external workshops
- I am fluent in communication with people across different competencies, cultures and skillsets.
- I am engaged in hiring as an interviewer.
- I suggest and drive improvements beyond my area of responsibility.

## Am I doing good?
- I am universally considered a go-to person for open-ended tasks.
- I am not evading routine tasks.
- “Self-growth by helping others grow” mindset.
- Acceptance of tradeoffs and workarounds in difficult situations.
- Striving for excellence.
- I am engaged in public activities on Business Analysis

## Behavior to avoid
- “If you want something done right, do it yourself” aficionado.
- Overcomplicating solutions.
- Haughtiness.
