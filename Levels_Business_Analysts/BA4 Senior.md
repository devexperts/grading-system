# Senior Business Analyst
Senior BA independently handles features of any complexity and contributes to department development.

A Senior BA has a deep understanding and wide experience with Software Development and FinTech business domain, thus he/she is able to communicate on the same language with both external stakeholders and Devexperts internal teams. Senior BA is ready and capable to handle any business case, support customers in their needs and facilitate the discussions to achieve the best solutions. Complete independence on a relatively big project/s, absence of escalations regarding BA work, and a proven track of complex features praised by customers are good signs that the BA matches the Senior grade.

Moreover, Senior BA is sharing his knowledge and contributing to department professional growth.

Typical tasks for a Senior would cover designing solutions for vaguely-defined requirements and use cases, advise on product improvements based on industry best-practices, investigate complex incidents, establish requirements documentation practices in Delivery Unit, all that on top of routine activities done by Middle. There are also multiple ways a Senior can contribute to BA department and Delivery Unit - either by improving onboarding materials, or by mentoring other BAs, or by setting up educational activities, such as presentations.

Senior grade is a solid achievement, the further career path can be either extending technical and domain knowledge so that a Senior would be able to cover adjacent roles such as PM or Solution Architect and work on advanced products and solutions. Alternatively, a Senior can be taking the responsibility to onboard and guide the newly formed BA teams for larger accounts to ensure knowledge transfer and operational stability of a project. Both variants can be mixed depending on personal aspirations, and Lead grade would be an acknowledgement of further exceptional contributions.

## Overview and regular tasks
- I can drive implementation of any complex feature from its concept to customer demo. I have a proven track of delivered complex features.
- I am providing and maintaining high-quality requirements that are well understood by customer, developers and qa.
- I am handling complex communications and customers’ negative feedback.
- I am proactively interfering if the implementation or testing process is stuck at some point
- I am contributing to department activities (mentoring, onboarding or presentations)

## Am I doing good?
- I am owning the scope of the project/s.
- Internal teams trust my decisions.
- Customer actively contacts me for any kind of questions.
- I understand overall system architecture and can provide an opinion on general architectural topics.
- I demonstrate excellent domain knowledge.
- I have a “yes” mindset
- I have a positive effect on the team spirit.
- I am improving my soft-skills and learning more about the cultures of my teammates and customers.
- English level B2+

## Behavior to avoid 
- Arrogance.
- Indifference to business needs.
- Criticism without making suggestions
- Motivation depends on external circumstances
- Overpromising
