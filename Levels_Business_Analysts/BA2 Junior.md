# Junior Business Analyst

Junior BA elicits a real need behind customer request and can properly structure and document the requirements.

A Junior BA is someone who has a basic understanding of BA role, Business Analysis methodology and techniques, their place in the Software Development Lifecycle. Junior knows the processes established in Devexperts and following them allows him/her to consistently contribute on routine tasks.

Junior shall be familiar with basic Domain terminology and main functionalities of the product he is working on.

Typical tasks for Junior would be to document requirements provided by stakeholders, existing processes, meeting notes, organize documented requirements, act as a shadow for other BAs, help to resolve minor/normal issues and, of course, learn.

Good progressing Junior would proactively gather the information he needs to complete his tasks, extend his expertise and area of responsibility.

As the Junior gets sufficient knowledge and starts acting independently, a promotion to Middle BA would occur.

## Overview and regular tasks
- I can follow the process for requirements elicitation and documentation.
- I attend team activities and can clearly articulate my status and possible blockers to the team.
- I can document a feature on all of Business Requirements/System Requirements/Solution specification level. I can navigate existing documentation.
- I learn more about the devexperts products and business domain.

## Am I doing good?
- Developers and QA are not avoiding asking me questions.
- I am proactively contacting stakeholders and scheduling meetings to address questions or issues.
- I am proactively seeking guidance on my tasks.
- I am proactively seeking new tasks.
- I know people who can answer my questions.
- I can accept feedback towards my performance.

## Behavior to avoid
- Lack of self-motivation.
- Not signaling difficulties.
- Not doing your own research.
- Not showcasing your progress.
