# Middle Business Analyst
Middle BA drives the customer and internal teams towards finding and implementing the optimal solution for moderately complex feature.

A Middle BA has some experience in Devexperts processes and sufficient understanding of the Business Domain related to his project. His knowledge and experience allows him to act independently and cover all requests in a limited area of responsibility. For the most complex topics, a Middle still needs assistance from a senior colleague.

Typical tasks for a Middle would be to gather high-level requirements/feedbacks and suggest solutions, leading grooming calls on features, showing demos and helping customer with some inquiries on product functionality.

After getting hands-on experience in different projects, and extending his knowledge of business domain in both width and depth, a Middle would be able to address requests of any complexity, which would mark his transition to the Senior grade.

## Overview and regular tasks
- I can drive implementation of a typical feature from its concept to customer demo with no supervision.
- I manage the expectations regarding my deadlines and workload.
- I am a primary contact point for the customer regarding specific functional areas.  
- I am a primary contact point for internal teams to clarify questions in my areas of responsibility.
- I am proactively taking new tasks.

## Am I doing good?
- I suggest solutions when asking questions.
- I am challenging customers’ requests.
- I am challenging my team’s technical solutions and estimates.
- I am reliable in my commitments
- I have good relationships with key people in my team.

## Behavior to avoid
- Not following company/project rules and procedures.
- Being too formal and reverting to “ticket reassigner” role.
- Lack of initiative
- Know-all attitude.
- Avoiding complex topics.
